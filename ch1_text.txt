Chapter Overview
This chapter explains the motivation behind Project Hades, the problem it addresses, and the
long-term vision that guides every engineering and product decision.
Why Hades Exists
Hades is an AI Operating System (AI OS) designed to become the primary interface between humans,
computers, software, and artificial intelligence. Unlike today's AI assistants, Hades is not designed to
answer prompts. Its purpose is to understand goals, collaborate with users as an equal partner, and
autonomously coordinate software, AI models, and digital workflows. The user focuses on what they want
to accomplish. Hades determines how to accomplish it.
Vision Statement
Hades transforms human intent into autonomous execution while preserving human control over
goals and decisions.

The Problem
Modern AI tools are incredibly capable. However, users must still coordinate them manually. A typical
workflow today looks like this:
Need Research
Gemini
Need Code
Claude
Need Images
ChatGPT
Need Notes
Notion

Google Calendar
Need Email
Gmail
The human becomes the operating system. Users constantly decide which AI to use, move information
between tools, maintain context, repeat themselves, manage workflows, review outputs, and coordinate
everything.
Context Switching - Users constantly move between tools.
Prompt Repetition - The same context is repeatedly copied into different systems.
Workflow Management - Humans manually decide which tool to use, what order to use them, and how
outputs connect.
Lost Context - Every AI starts over. Long-term collaboration barely exists.
Decision Fatigue - The user manages technology instead of solving problems.
Today's AI helps people perform work.
Hades helps people manage work.
Why This Matters
The current market is dominated by fragmented AI tools. That fragmentation creates a hidden tax on
productivity: the user becomes the coordinator, and coordination itself becomes the bottleneck.

North Star
Project Hades is not intended to become another AI application. Its long-term objective is to become an
operating layer for intelligence. Applications become capabilities. AI models become implementation details.
The relationship remains constant.
North Star
Hades exists to remove the burden of orchestrating intelligence, allowing humans to focus
entirely on thinking, creating, and deciding.

Chapter Summary
• Hades is an AI Operating System, not an AI assistant.
• Users communicate goals instead of managing tools.
• Hades coordinates intelligence on behalf of the user.
• Every design decision is guided by the North Star.
• The vision prioritizes partnership, autonomy, and trust over feature count.
